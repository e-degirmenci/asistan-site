Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Asistan is a personal, non-commercial application used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

With the owner's explicit consent, Asistan accesses the owner's Gmail account with read-only permission (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete or modify emails.

How data is used

  • Recent emails are read once a day to create a short summary for the owner.
  • Email content is sent to the Anthropic Claude API solely to generate this summary.
  • Only the generated summaries are stored, in a private database accessible only to the owner. Full email contents are not stored.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Asistan's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account settings under "Third-party apps & services".
